## Postdoctoral Researcher or Assistant Researcher - Bioinformatics and Evolutionary Genetics (2 positions)

**Responsibilities**

- Develop and optimize single-cell lineage-tracing and multi-omic integration workflows.
- Integrate single-cell multi-omics, spatial transcriptomics, and genomic variation from longitudinal clinical cohorts to resolve tumor clonal evolution.
- Help manage the high-performance computing cluster and lead or contribute to research-funding applications.

**Qualifications**

- PhD completed or nearing completion in bioinformatics, computational biology, evolutionary biology, biostatistics, computer science, or a related field.
- Proficiency in Linux, Python, and R with strong high-throughput omics experience.
- Experience in single-cell or spatial omics, phylogenetic inference, population-genetic methods, or algorithm development is preferred.
- First-author or co-first-author research paper in a relevant field, published or submitted.
- Strong independent research, scientific reasoning, writing, and teamwork skills.

## Postdoctoral Researcher or Assistant Researcher - Cell, Molecular, and Cancer Biology (2 positions)

**Responsibilities**

- Develop and optimize single-cell lineage-tracing systems and experimental methods.
- Build genetically engineered mouse models, patient-derived xenografts, and patient-derived organoids for colorectal, lung, and other cancers.
- Establish models of radiation resistance, drug resistance, and recurrence.
- Independently design experiments, analyze data, write manuscripts, and apply for funding.

**Qualifications**

- PhD completed or nearing completion in cell biology, molecular biology, oncology, genetics, basic medicine, biomedicine, or a related field.
- Experience in molecular cloning, lentiviral packaging, flow sorting, and orthotopic or metastatic mouse models.
- Gene editing, lineage tracing, or tumor-model expertise is preferred.
- First-author or co-first-author paper in oncology or cell biology, published or submitted.

## Wet-lab Technician (1 position)

Responsibilities include routine molecular and cell biology, sequencing-library construction, clinical sample organization, PDO biobanking, mouse breeding and genotyping, purchasing, instrument maintenance, and laboratory safety. Applicants should hold at least a bachelor's degree in biology, medicine, pharmacy, veterinary medicine, bioengineering, or a related discipline. Cell culture, molecular cloning, or mouse-handling experience is preferred.

## Support and development

- Competitive compensation. The recruitment announcement specifies **CNY 350,000-550,000 annual compensation for postdoctoral appointments**; final packages follow institutional policy.
- Support for applications to national and provincial research funds and talent programs.
- Outstanding postdoctoral researchers may be recommended for permanent institutional research appointments after completion.
- Outstanding research assistants and technicians may be recommended for graduate study with the lab or collaborating institutions.
- Paid annual leave, union benefits, staff health examinations, and assistance with eligible housing or talent subsidies according to institutional and local policy.
- Access to clinical samples, molecular biology, high-throughput sequencing, high-performance computing, and animal facilities.

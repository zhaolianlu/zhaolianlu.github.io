Dr. Zhaolian Lu is a Research Professor at Shandong Cancer Hospital. She received doctoral training in Life Sciences in the Department of Biology at Saint Louis University under Professor Zhenguo Lin, and completed postdoctoral research in Professor Zheng Hu's laboratory at the Shenzhen Institute of Advanced Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences. Before joining Shandong Cancer Hospital in October 2026, she held Assistant Professor, Associate Professor, and Young Research Professor appointments at the Shenzhen Institute of Advanced Technology.

Her research integrates single-cell lineage tracing, single-cell multi-omics, spatial transcriptomics, animal models, and computational biology to reconstruct tumor evolution from normal cells to malignant transformation and from primary tumors to distant metastases.
